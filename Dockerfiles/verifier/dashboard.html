Why this cannot be faked

Every attestation sends a fresh 128-bit random nonce to the TPM, which signs the PCR state using its hardware-bound Attestation Key. The verifier checks the signature with tpm2_checkquote and independently recomputes PCR 10 from the binary IMA log.
